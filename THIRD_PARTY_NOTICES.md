## Lefff — Lexique des formes fléchies du français

The verb data of the conjugation path is derived from the Lefff, by Benoît Sagot (Inria),
distributed under the LGPL-LR licence (Lesser General Public License for Linguistic Resources),
through the `french-verbs-lefff` package.

Derived files, regenerated by `pnpm --filter @little-tables/verbs generate` and
`cargo run -p lt-domain --example extract-irregular-verbs`:

- `crates/lt-domain/data/verbs-irregular.tsv`
- `crates/lt-domain/data/verbs-index.tsv`
- `crates/lt-domain/tests/fixtures/lefff-forms.json.gz`
- `apps/app/public/verbs/index.json`

Reviewed corrections are applied to the Lefff before these files are written
(`tools/verbs/src/generate.ts`, for example « je peux » rather than « je puis »).
