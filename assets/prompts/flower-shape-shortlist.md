# Flower Shape Shortlist

Status: approved concept shortlist

These nine flowers are the approved inventory for future garden artwork:

- Rose lotus
- Twilight lupine
- Velvet foxglove
- Plum snapdragon
- Sunset zinnia
- Ruby bleeding heart
- Blushing peony
- Ivory magnolia
- Blue wisteria

## Direction

- Preserve the distinct silhouette of each flower instead of reusing the existing tulip or daisy heads.
- Keep the current friendly, outlined garden style and shared pot language.
- Treat color as a skin; the flower's silhouette should remain recognizable without color.
- Design dormant, growing, and mature states from the same underlying plant structure.

## Discarded Concepts

Do not carry forward the other concepts from the visual explorations, including Tiger lily,
Scarlet poppy, Ivory calla, Coral protea, Winter snowdrop, Sunbird strelitzia, Crimson hibiscus,
Azure cornflower, Coral anthurium, and Silver thistle.
